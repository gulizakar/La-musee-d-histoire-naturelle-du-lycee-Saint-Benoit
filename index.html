A locked cabinet, opened again

For decades this collection sat sealed in storage. Piece by piece, it has been cleaned, identified, logged and photographed — and is now open to anyone curious enough to look closely.

Two ways to look

Browse the collection as a catalogue, specimen by specimen — or follow the tree of life to see how everything in the collection is related.

Who did this

This project is the work of one student, with a great deal of help. The full story is on the About and Behind the Scenes pages.

About

[Your name] is a student who spent [timeframe] restoring a natural history collection that had been in storage for years. This site is the record of that work — what was found, what it took to save it, and what it means to open it up.

This project began with a single drawer of unlabeled specimens and a question: what happens to a collection nobody is looking after? What follows is an attempt to answer that — through cleaning, identifying, logging, and photographing every specimen the cabinet held, and building a place where anyone can see the result.
